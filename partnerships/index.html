GOLD Center / Partnerships

Partnerships

Knowledge through institutional cooperation.

GOLD Center works with national, regional and international organizations and professional institutions in areas of common interest, subject to each cooperation arrangement.

PJL — Peace Journalism Lab

Cooperation in professional learning, dialogue and initiatives falling within areas of mutual expertise and institutional interest.

UNICRI

Cooperation related to crime prevention, criminal justice, education, training and capacity-building, in accordance with the applicable cooperation arrangements.

References to partner organizations describe cooperation arrangements and do not imply endorsement beyond their agreed scope.

Approach to cooperation

Partnerships built around defined areas of mutual interest

GOLD Center approaches institutional cooperation on a project-by-project basis, with the scope of each relationship defined by the relevant agreement, programme or activity.

Depending on the arrangement, cooperation may involve professional education, research, policy dialogue, expert exchange, technical assistance, conferences or other capacity-building activities within the Center’s areas of work.

Institutional programmes

Joint or tailored professional learning around agreed objectives and audiences.

Research & dialogue

Collaborative research, expert discussion and knowledge activities in areas of common interest.

Expert exchange

Capacity-building

Professional and institutional development initiatives designed around identified needs.

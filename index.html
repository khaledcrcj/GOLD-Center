About GOLD

An integrated platform for knowledge and institutional development

G.O.L.D. Center works at the intersection of governance, oversight, law and diplomacy, connecting professional knowledge with institutional practice.

The Center supports public institutions, judicial and prosecution authorities, law-enforcement bodies, regulatory and audit authorities, diplomatic and governmental institutions, corporations, universities, legal professionals, students and early-career professionals through specialized training, capacity-building, research, policy dialogue, technical assistance and collaborative initiatives.

GOLD also develops tailored programmes in response to institutional needs, bringing together carefully selected experts according to the subject, audience and objectives of each engagement.

Capacity-Building & Technical Assistance

From concept to implementation.

GOLD supports institutions, international organizations, development partners, corporations and other stakeholders in designing and implementing capacity-building and technical-assistance initiatives across Egypt.

Depending on project requirements, G.O.L.D. can support programme design, expert identification, curriculum development, training delivery, participant coordination, training materials, reporting, translation and interpretation, communications and on-ground implementation.

Through its training facilities and trusted partners, G.O.L.D. can also arrange venues, transportation, travel and logistical support in Cairo and other locations across Egypt.

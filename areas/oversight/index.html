O · Oversight

Regulatory Oversight, Audit, Compliance & Accountability

Effective oversight strengthens accountability, integrity, resilience and institutional trust across the public and private sectors.

Oversight in Practice

Assurance, control and accountability.

GOLD addresses the systems and professional functions through which institutions monitor risk, assess controls, ensure compliance, investigate misconduct and provide assurance over governance and performance.

Regulatory & Institutional Oversight

Regulators, supervisory authorities, inspection bodies, public audit institutions and risk-based supervision.

Internal Audit & Assurance

Internal audit functions, audit planning, risk-based auditing, assurance, audit committees and professional practice.

Risk Management & Internal Control

Enterprise risk, internal-control frameworks, control testing and organizational resilience.

Compliance, Ethics & Integrity

Regulatory and corporate compliance, ethics, conflicts of interest, whistleblowing and anti-corruption controls.

Fraud, Investigations & Forensic Practice

Fraud-risk assessment, detection, institutional investigations, forensic accounting, evidence handling and investigative methods.

Technology, Cyber & AI Assurance

Auditing AI, digital systems, cybersecurity, data governance and technology risk.

Professional Context

Public oversight and corporate assurance.

The Oversight pillar is relevant to regulatory authorities, audit and inspection bodies, investigative and law-enforcement professionals, internal auditors, compliance teams, audit committees and corporate-control functions.

Areas of work

Four pillars. One connected approach.

Governance, Oversight, Law and Diplomacy provide the substantive framework for GOLD Center’s programmes, research, dialogue and technical activities.

G

Governance

Institutional development, public policy, decision-making, digital governance and responsible AI governance.

O

Oversight

Accountability, integrity, anti-corruption, compliance, transparency and institutional oversight.

L

Law

Rule of law, justice and security, international cooperation and legal issues arising from emerging technologies.

D

Diplomacy

Diplomatic practice, international affairs, negotiation, mediation, protocol and international organizations.

Integrated approach

Why the four pillars belong together

The four GOLD pillars are distinct, but many real institutional challenges sit at their intersection. A digital-governance issue may involve legal rules and oversight controls; an international justice matter may require both legal cooperation and diplomatic engagement.

The Center therefore uses the pillars as a connected framework for designing programmes, research and institutional activities around the problem to be addressed rather than around disciplinary boundaries alone.

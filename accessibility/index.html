Accessibility Statement

G.O.L.D. Center aims to make its website usable by the widest practical range of visitors.

We aim to use clear structure, readable typography, keyboard-accessible navigation, meaningful headings, alternative text for important images and responsive layouts.

If you experience an accessibility difficulty or need website information in another practical format, please contact info@goldc.org and identify the page or content concerned.

Accessibility will be reviewed as the website develops and as new functions, media and programme content are introduced.

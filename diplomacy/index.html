Applied focus

Diplomacy as professional practice and institutional engagement

Diplomacy requires a combination of substantive knowledge, communication, negotiation, protocol awareness and an understanding of the institutions and legal frameworks that shape international engagement.

GOLD Center can bring these dimensions together through practice-oriented learning, simulations, expert dialogue and comparative perspectives relevant to diplomats, public officials, professionals and emerging practitioners.

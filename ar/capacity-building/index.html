بناء القدرات والمساعدة الفنية

من الفكرة إلى التنفيذ.

GOLD supports institutions and development partners in designing and implementing capacity-building and technical-assistance initiatives across Egypt.

بناء القدرات المؤسسية

تنمية قدرات مستدامة.

GOLD can design structured capacity-building interventions around identified institutional needs, target groups and desired outcomes.

Needs & programme design

Defining objectives, audiences, learning outcomes and implementation approach.

Expert selection

Identifying practitioners, academics and specialists suited to the engagement.

التدريب & development

Courses, workshops, simulations, executive sessions and other learning formats.

Implementation support

Participant coordination, materials, reporting and project follow-up.

المساعدة الفنية

دعم متخصص للأولويات المؤسسية.

Technical assistance may include expert advice, policy and legal support, programme development, institutional analysis, research, workshops and implementation assistance, depending on the engagement.

المشروعات الممولة

شريك محلي لتنفيذ المبادرات الممولة.

GOLD welcomes engagement with international organizations, development agencies, public institutions and other funding partners seeking to implement training, capacity-building, research or technical-assistance projects in Egypt.

GOLD can support substantive project requirements, including design, expert deployment, curriculum and learning content, as well as operational delivery.

Project arrangements can include venue coordination, transportation, participant logistics, interpretation and translation, materials, communications, event support and reporting, directly or through trusted service partners as appropriate.

Discuss a Funded Project

فرص الائتلافات والشراكات التعاونية

مقترحات مشتركة وتنفيذ تعاوني.

GOLD is open to consortium participation, joint proposals and collaborative funding applications with institutions whose expertise and objectives complement the Center’s areas of work.

Organizations seeking a local implementation partner in Egypt may engage GOLD for substantive expertise, professional training, local coordination, institutional engagement and logistical support.

Discuss a Consortium Opportunity

تنفيذ المشروعات في مصر

دعم في القاهرة ومختلف أنحاء مصر.

Through its training facilities and trusted partners, GOLD can arrange venues, transportation and other delivery requirements in locations suited to the project.

The exact division of responsibilities, third-party services and project deliverables is agreed for each engagement.

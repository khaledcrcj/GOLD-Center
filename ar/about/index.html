عن GOLD

مؤسسة مصرية بامتداد دولي.

مركز GOLD منصة متعددة التخصصات مكرسة للارتقاء بالمعرفة المهنية والقدرات المؤسسية والخبرة التطبيقية في مجالات الحوكمة والرقابة والقانون والدبلوماسية.

Our Role

معرفة مرتبطة بالممارسة.

GOLD combines professional education, capacity-building, applied research, policy dialogue, technical assistance and collaborative initiatives within one institutional platform.

The Center works with public institutions, justice and law-enforcement bodies, regulators and audit authorities, diplomatic and governmental institutions, corporations, universities, professionals, students and early-career professionals.

Its work is grounded in the belief that high-quality professional knowledge is most valuable when it can be translated into institutional practice.

Vision

مؤسسات أقوى من خلال المعرفة والخبرة والممارسة المسؤولة.

GOLD seeks to contribute to stronger, more capable and more accountable institutions across Egypt, the Middle East and Africa by connecting contemporary knowledge with practical professional expertise.

Mission

لتطوير الأفراد والمؤسسات والممارسة المهنية.

Professional education

Deliver rigorous, practice-oriented learning across the GOLD pillars.

Institutional capacity

Support institutions in developing sustainable professional and organizational capability.

Applied knowledge

Connect research, comparative experience and expert dialogue with real institutional needs.

Collaboration

Build effective local and international partnerships around shared objectives.

Our Expert Model

خبرات يتم اختيارها بحسب الاحتياج.

GOLD engages practitioners, academics and subject-matter specialists according to the needs of each programme, project or institutional engagement.

Rather than relying on a single fixed faculty for every subject, GOLD identifies experts whose experience matches the topic, audience and intended outcomes of the engagement.

برنامجs may be delivered directly by GOLD experts or in cooperation with partner institutions. The relevant programme page will identify delivery and certification arrangements where applicable.

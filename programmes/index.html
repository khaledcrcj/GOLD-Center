Programme design

Flexible formats for different audiences and institutional objectives

GOLD Center programmes may be delivered as short courses, workshops, executive sessions, seminars, simulations, professional development series or tailored institutional programmes. The format, level and duration depend on the learning objectives and target audience.

Programme development can combine substantive expertise with case-based learning, comparative practice and practical exercises, particularly where professional application is central to the subject.

Open professional programmes

Programmes designed for individual professionals and participants from multiple institutions.

Institutional programmes

Tailored learning developed for a specific institution, team or professional group.

Student & early-career learning

Applied learning that helps connect academic study with professional environments.

Executive & specialist formats

Focused sessions for senior professionals, specialists and decision-makers.

Knowledge in practice

Research that supports professional and institutional discussion

GOLD Center’s knowledge work is intended to make research usable. Topics may be examined through legal analysis, policy research, comparative institutional practice, expert contributions and structured professional dialogue.

Outputs may support training, institutional discussion, professional awareness or further research, depending on the nature of the project and cooperation arrangement.

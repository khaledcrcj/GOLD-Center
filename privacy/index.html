Privacy Policy

This policy explains, at a general level, how information submitted through the G.O.L.D. Center website may be handled.

Information we collect

Information may be collected when you submit an enquiry, register interest in a programme, submit an expert profile or otherwise contact G.O.L.D. Center. This may include contact information, professional information, messages, programme interests and documents that you choose to provide.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, administer expressions of interest, assess expert profiles, communicate about relevant programmes or opportunities, and support legitimate institutional and administrative activities.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed through service providers used to operate website forms, communications or related services. Information may also be shared with relevant delivery partners where necessary for a specific programme or engagement and where appropriate.

Retention and security

GOLD seeks to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to apply appropriate administrative and technical safeguards. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your enquiries

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact info@goldc.org.

This policy should be reviewed against applicable legal requirements before the website begins collecting substantial volumes of personal data or introducing additional analytics, marketing or account features.

About the Center

Where expertise meets practice.

GOLD Center is a multidisciplinary platform connecting governance, oversight, law and diplomacy with professional education, research and institutional needs.

Mission & approach

A practice-oriented regional platform

GOLD stands for Governance, Oversight, Law and Diplomacy. These four complementary pillars frame the Center’s work in professional education, institutional development, research, policy dialogue, technical assistance and applied expertise.

The Center works with and for judicial bodies, prosecution authorities, law-enforcement agencies, diplomatic and governmental institutions, universities, professionals and the private sector, according to the scope of each programme or cooperation arrangement.

Professional relevance

Programmes and knowledge activities are designed around practical institutional and professional needs.

Regional perspective

The Center focuses particularly on Egypt, the Middle East and Africa while drawing on comparative and international experience.

Expert network

Practitioners, academics and subject-matter specialists contribute practical, academic and policy perspectives.

Institutional identity

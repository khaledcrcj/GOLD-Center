Law in practice: justice, security and international cooperation

The Law pillar connects substantive legal knowledge with the institutional realities of justice administration, criminal justice, law enforcement, international cooperation and technology-driven legal change.

Programmes and knowledge activities may draw on national, comparative and international legal frameworks while remaining focused on professional application, institutional coordination and emerging operational challenges.
